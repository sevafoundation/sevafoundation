Support Us

We believe that every contribution, no matter how small, can create a ripple effect of positive change in our community. Your support is vital to our mission of "Empowering underprivileged Women and children through education", "Promoting environmental sustainability", "Improving Healthcare", "Providing disaster management", "Supporting livelihoods", Together we can make a meaningful impact in these critical areas and create a better future for our communities.


How You Can Help:

Make a Donation
Your financial support allows us to continue our vital work. Donations help fund our programs, provide resources, and reach more individuals in need.

Sponsor a Project
We have various projects that need sponsorship. By choosing to sponsor a specific initiative, you can make a direct impact on the lives of those we serve.

In-Kind Donations
We welcome in-kind donations such as clothes, food, educational materials. Your generosity can help us provide essential resources to those in need.

Corporate Partnerships
If you represent a business, consider partnering with us. Together, we can create meaningful change while enhancing your corporate social responsibility (CSR initiatives).


Get Involved:

There are many ways to get involved with Seva Foundation and make a difference in the lives of others. Whether you have a few hours to spare or want to commit long-term, your involvement is invaluable.

Become a Volunteer
Join our dedicated team of volunteers who work tirelessly to support our mission. We offer various roles that match your skills and interests, from hands-on work in the field to administrative support.

Internships
Gain valuable experience while contributing to meaningful projects. Our internships provide hands-on learning opportunities in areas such as project management, community outreach, fundraising and many more.

Host a Fundraising Event
Organize a fundraising event in your community to raise awareness and support for our cause. We can provide you with resources and guidance to make your event a success.

Spread the Word
Help us reach a wider audience by sharing our mission with your friends, family, and social networks. Follow us on social media and share our posts to spread our message.

Join Our Advocacy Efforts
Stay informed about issues related to our cause and participate in advocacy campaigns. Your voice can help influence change at the local, national, and global levels.

Thank you for considering supporting Seva Foundation. Together, we can create a brighter future for those in need. For more information or to discuss how you can get involved, please contact us at sevafoundationngo2022@gmail.com
